# Quick-Start Guide

## Hardware setup

Protect your Wallbox using a differential circuit breaker. Connect your Wallbox to your electric box.

## Wallbox Configuration

### Step 1: Connect

Connect to the Wallbox Hotpoint.

![WiFi QR](qr-wifi.svg)

From your computer, connect to the WiFi:

- Network: **Wallbox-Hotspot**
- Password: **Wallbox1**

### Step 2: Configure

Open the Wallbox configuration page.

![Wallbox Setup](qr-setup.svg)

From your computer, visit: <http://192.168.4.1>
